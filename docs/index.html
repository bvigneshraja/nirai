Features

Everything a merchant needs

From recording a sale to tracking who owes you money — Nirai covers the full business cycle.

📊

Live Dashboard

Total revenue, gross margin, outstanding balances, per-day margin trend, and product-wise margin breakdown — all at a glance.

🛒

Sales Entry

Record multi-item sales with automatic total calculation. See cost/unit, selling price, margin/unit, and total margin per line.

📦

Purchases

Log stock-in from suppliers. Stock levels update automatically. View per-product purchase history with dates and prices.

👥

Customer Management

Maintain a full customer directory with contact info, credit limits, and location. Search and sort instantly.

💰

Customer Balance

Track outstanding amounts per customer. See total owed, total paid, and pending balance with payment history.

⚠️

Low Stock Alerts

Products falling below threshold are surfaced on the dashboard automatically. Never run out of stock unexpectedly.

Access Control

Two roles, one system

Give your staff access without giving away sensitive data.

SUPER_ADMIN

Owner / Super Admin

Full access to all data and operations.

  • View full phone numbers
  • Add, edit, delete customers
  • Manage products & pricing
  • Record purchases & sales
  • View all reports & margins
ADMIN

Staff / Admin

Read access with sensitive data masked.

  • Phone numbers masked (****1234)
  • Cannot edit or delete customers
  • Can view all reports
  • Can record sales & purchases
  • Dashboard fully visible
